Xrash 0.4.6 fixes a sheet that changed size as you moved through it.

- On an iPad or a Mac, the Symbols sheet grew taller when it opened a symbol list, whose search field sits under the title, and stayed that tall after going back. Every sheet — Symbols, Saved, Settings, Report Crash and the welcome — now keeps the size it opened at.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed; after unzipping, clear the quarantine bit once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Xrash.app`.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware, and `Xrash-0.4.6-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
