Xrash 0.5.7 reads better in Chinese.

- The Simplified and Traditional Chinese translations read more naturally and use the same words as the system, one word for each thing throughout the app.
- The welcome screen says it is reading the reports the system has already written, instead of the reports "on this device", which is true on a Mac as well.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed; after unzipping, clear the quarantine bit once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Xrash.app`.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware, and `Xrash-0.5.7-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
