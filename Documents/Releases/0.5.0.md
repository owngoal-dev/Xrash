Xrash 0.5.0 lets you choose which reports send notifications.

- **Allow Notifications** replaces the old **Crash Notifications** switch and controls all notification categories. Turning it off keeps your category choices for next time.
- **Settings → Notifications → Notification Categories** groups the individual switches into crashes, hangs, resource limits, and analytics and logs. CPU usage, wakeups, and disk writes each have their own switch.
- Crashes and hangs are enabled by default; other categories are off until you choose them. Updating preserves the existing master switch and other preferences.
- Background notifications use the report's actual type before applying the category switches, even when its filename suggests a crash. Muted reports stay quiet when their category is enabled later, and resource logs no longer use up the limit for crash notifications.
- All new settings and explanatory text are translated into all thirteen supported languages.
- Built with icli 0.7.16.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed; after unzipping, clear the quarantine bit once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Xrash.app`.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware, and `Xrash-0.5.0-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
