Xrash 0.4.4 is the release that makes the app usable with VoiceOver: every icon-only button says what it does, a row reads as the one sentence it looks like, and what was only drawn is now spoken.

- Icon-only buttons were announced as a bare "button": the menu buttons on a report's rows, the filter button over the report list and the menu button in the text viewer each say what they open now.
- A report row, a saved bundle, a process in the inbox and a stack frame were read one piece at a time, separators and all; each is one stop that reads as a sentence.
- A frame or register row that reads as one sentence still offers its menu: the same items, in the same order, are in VoiceOver's actions.
- A checkmark was all that said a symbol archive had already been imported; the row says Imported.
- The crash report's title field and its notes had no names of their own, and the row that adds a suggested process never said it adds one.
- A long import read out a percentage with nothing to say what it was measuring; the bar is named.
- Chevrons, plus signs, empty-state pictures and a text view's placeholder were stops of their own on the way through a screen, and are out of the way now.
- The two new words, Filter and Imported, are translated in all thirteen languages.
- The build now refuses a label VoiceOver would never read, so no row can go quiet again.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed; after unzipping, clear the quarantine bit once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Xrash.app`.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware, and `Xrash-0.4.4-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
