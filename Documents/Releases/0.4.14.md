Xrash 0.4.14 centres the report's icon on iOS 26.

- On iPadOS 26 the report's icon sits over the middle of the report again, not toward the list beside it. The spinner on the Raw view is centred the same way.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed; after unzipping, clear the quarantine bit once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Xrash.app`.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware, and `Xrash-0.4.14-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
