Xrash 0.4.12 fixes the selection toolbar on iOS 15, 16 and 17.

- While selecting reports, Delete All and Select All now sit directly above the tab bar. Before, the toolbar floated a bar's height too high, with reports showing through beneath it (#6).

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed; after unzipping, clear the quarantine bit once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Xrash.app`.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware, and `Xrash-0.4.12-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
