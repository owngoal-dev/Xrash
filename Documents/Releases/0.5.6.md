Xrash 0.5.6 is made for iPhone Duo.

- Xrash is built against the iOS 27.1 SDK. On iPhone Duo it fills the display, and its tabs and the report's buttons run down the side under the status bar, instead of the app sitting in the middle of the screen.
- Opening or closing the device keeps the report you were reading. It moves between the Reports tab and the column beside the list, at the same place and in the same view.
- Line numbers in a report's text follow the width of its column rather than the kind of device, so they show on the wide inner display and stay off where the column is narrow.
- The welcome screens keep their buttons clear of the side the bar takes, and "Not Now" sits in the middle of the space that is left.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed; after unzipping, clear the quarantine bit once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Xrash.app`.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware, and `Xrash-0.5.6-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
