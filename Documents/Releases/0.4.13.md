Xrash 0.4.13 orders the report list by what happened last.

- When reports are sorted by date, each section now sits by its newest report, so the section that crashed most recently comes first. Sorting by name keeps the fixed category order.
- Section headers show only their name; the report count is gone.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed; after unzipping, clear the quarantine bit once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Xrash.app`.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware, and `Xrash-0.4.13-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
