Xrash 0.4.5 fixes one sheet that would not hold still.

- The welcome opened from Settings on an iPad or a Mac is a sheet over a sheet, and it jumped in size when it opened and again on every page it turned to. It now keeps one size from start to finish.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed; after unzipping, clear the quarantine bit once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Xrash.app`.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware, and `Xrash-0.4.5-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
