Xrash 0.4.16 announces a crash when it happens on roothide.

- On roothide, a crash is announced a few seconds after it happens, with Xrash closed. Until now the notification waited until Xrash was next opened: the bootstrap's launchctl moved the report directories the helper watches into a folder that never exists, so nothing started it.
- A run of crashes close together is announced in full and on time.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed; after unzipping, clear the quarantine bit once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine Xrash.app`.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide or `iphoneos-arm64` for rootless firmware, and `Xrash-0.4.16-macos.zip` for a Mac. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS`.
